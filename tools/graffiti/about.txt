GRAFFITI WALL build 1  -  a demo for the Octophonic 64
======================================================

A full-screen brick wall - your own photo of your wall - with a big graffiti scroller sprayed on it: hot pink to orange to yellow
bubble letters with a thick dark outline, a white highlight streak, paint drips and a soft shadow on the bricks. The text scrolls
right to left and bobs a little: "OCTOPHONIC 64 ... 8 SIDS ... OCTOVIC 256 ... SPRAYED ON NEAL'S WALL ... MONG00SE WOZ ERE ..."
and round again (about 70 seconds a lap). Silent (a hook for a tune is built in).

How it is done. The wall is 35 sprites of 64 x 64 in 256 colours, behind everything (a seamless tile made from the photo: the
courses straightened, the light evened out, the joins cross-faded). The lettering is a tile layer with a scrolling map; a second
layer, using the same map with grey tiles in "taken away" mode and shifted 7 pixels, is the shadow. The 6502 moves the scroll 2
pixels a frame and, every 4th frame, writes one new column of the map from the REU. Nothing is drawn by the PC.

Keys:  SPACE pauses and goes on.   F7 silences all 8 SIDs and quits to BASIC.

PUTTING IT ON THE MISTER (FILEZILLA)
------------------------------------
1. Find the MiSTer's IP address (the MiSTer menu, or your router's list).
2. Open FileZilla: protocol SFTP, host = the MiSTer's IP, user root, password 1, port 22.
3. On the MiSTer side open /media/fat/games/C64/Demos/ and make a new folder there, for example "Graffiti Wall build 1".
4. Put "Graffiti Wall.prg" and "Graffiti Wall.reu" together in that folder (the same name, so the Octophonic browser
   puts the .reu into the REU first, then runs the .prg).
5. Start the Octophonic 64 core, F1 (the browser), Demos, the folder, RETURN on "Graffiti Wall.prg".

The .reu is 0.8 MB: the browser shows a load bar, then the screen stays black for a moment while the 6502 copies it into OctoVIC's
memory, and the wall appears.

Needs the Octophonic 64 core with OctoVIC 2 or later; a stock C64 gets a message and nothing else.
