DNA WAVE - for the Octophonic 64 (OctoVIC 2+, 8 SIDs, REU). By Mong00se.

Pendulum Wave's maths and music, seen as a double helix. 24 base pairs turn about the helix's axis, each like one of
the 24 pendulums: rung i turns 30 + i times a minute. Because each turns a little faster than the last, the ladder
twists - flat at the start, a slow twist, tighter and tighter, a tangle - and after exactly 60 seconds it is flat again.

Each rung is one of the 24 SID voices (8 SIDs x 3) and rings its note as it turns edge-on, where the two strands cross:
a glow flashes there and the rung lights up. One great chord when they line up, then ripples, cascades and shimmer.

All live on the 6502: the turning (24-bit phases, so the minute comes round exactly), the sound, the beads of the two
strands (sprites, shaded and sized by depth - the near side in front of the rungs, the far side behind them), the
striped rungs (the blitter, into the back of two layers) and the glows (sprites added to what is under them). The REU
brings the graphics, the palette and the background - a night of out-of-focus lights.

Run it: put DNA Wave.prg and DNA Wave.reu in the same folder (or run "Install to MiSTer.bat"), then pick DNA Wave.prg
in the Octophonic browser.
