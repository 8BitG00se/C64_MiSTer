PENDULUM WAVE - for the Octophonic 64 (OctoVIC 2+, 8 SIDs, REU). By Mong00se.

24 pendulums hang in a row, each a little shorter than the one before: the first swings 30 times a minute, the next
31, and so on to 53. They start together; then they drift into a travelling snake, two waves, three, what looks like
chaos - and after exactly 60 seconds every one has swung a whole number of times and they all line up again.

Each pendulum is one of the 24 SID voices (8 SIDs x 3 voices). It rings its note - D minor pentatonic, the longest
pendulum lowest - as it swings through the bottom of its arc, so the pattern you see is the pattern you hear: one great
chord when they line up, then ripples, cascades and shimmer, and back to the chord.

All of it live on the 6502: each pendulum's phase (24 bits, so the minute comes round exactly), its swing and the rise
at the ends of its arc (OctoVIC's maths unit), the strings (the blitter, drawn into the back of two layers), the balls
and their glows (sprites; the glow is added to what is under it), and a light trail behind every ball that fades by
its palette colours. The REU brings the graphics and the palette.

Run it: put Pendulum Wave.prg and Pendulum Wave.reu in the same folder (or run "Install to MiSTer.bat"), then pick
Pendulum Wave.prg in the Octophonic browser.
