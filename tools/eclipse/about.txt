ECLIPSE BEACH build 1  -  a backdrop demo for the Octophonic 64
================================================================

The beach stage's backdrop for Mong00se's karate game, as a real 6502 program: an afternoon sky (an 8 x 8 dithered gradient), a low sun,
a sea with lit wave crests and the sun's glitter column, a still beach with wet sand and fine ripples - and a total solar eclipse as a
60 second time lapse. The moon is invisible until it covers the sun (then it is a black bite out of a deep orange disc), the light fails,
the sky goes to near-black at the top, indigo, and a band of sunset orange on the horizon; stars come out from the top down and twinkle;
a diamond ring flashes just before and just after totality; at totality the whole moon is black inside a thin pearly corona.
The sea and sand darken with the eclipse. After 60 seconds it starts again. No music in build 1.

Keys:  1 to 6 switch a layer off / on (1 picture: sky, sea, beach;  2 sun glow;  3 sun and moon;  4 corona and ring;  5 stars;  6 foam)
       L  shows the layer names at the bottom (gold = on, grey = off)       D  the debug line (LATE, MAX, TOT, OVR: 0 is right)
       SPACE pauses       F7 quits to BASIC.     Nothing is on screen unless you ask for it.

PUTTING IT ON THE MISTER
------------------------
Double-click "Install to MiSTer.bat": it copies this folder to C:\MiSTerBridge\Games\C64\Octophonic\Eclipse Beach (copies only, deletes nothing).
On the MiSTer: F1 (the browser), the folder, RETURN on "Eclipse Beach.prg" - the .reu of the same name (4.4 MB: the picture, the sprite
frames and 1500 ticks of palette and sprite table) goes into the REU first, the screen stays black for a second or two while the 6502
copies it into OctoVIC's memory, and the show starts.

"Eclipse Beach build 1.d64" has the program, its source "ECLIPSE.ASM" (for the OCTOCODE editor) and five OctoPaint pictures:
SKY.OCT, SEA.OCT, BEACH.OCT (the still layers as they look at the start), SUN FRAMES.OCT (30 frames of the sun and the moon's bite)
and CORONA.OCT (the corona and ring at three moments). The program on the disk still needs the .reu.

Needs the Octophonic 64 core with OctoVIC 2 or later and the 16 MB REU; a stock C64 gets a message and nothing else.

Made by Mong00se. 6502 (ACME); gen.py prepares the data on a PC. github.com/8BitG00se/C64_MiSTer, tools/eclipse.
