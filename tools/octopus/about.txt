OCTOPUS build 1  -  a demo for the Octophonic 64
================================================

The Octophonic octopus, turning and breathing in technicolour, with a twinkling night sky behind it. Silent (a hook for a tune is built in).

What it is. The octopus is one still picture on an OctoVIC bitmap layer that the machine itself turns and sizes: it makes one full turn
every 9 seconds and breathes in and out (85% to 115%). Its body is painted in swirling bands of palette colours, and the colour flows round
and through it because the copper rewrites 249 palette entries every frame, in the vertical blank (the way of the Black Hole demo). The
sky is the backdrop colour changing on every line, with 40 sprite stars that twinkle. The 6502 only hands OctoVIC the turn and zoom each
frame, and looks at the keys. The show loops after 36 seconds (4 turns, 2 breaths, 15 colour cycles).

Keys:  SPACE pauses (the colours stop too) and goes on.   F7 silences all 8 SIDs and quits to BASIC.

PUTTING IT ON THE MISTER (FILEZILLA)
------------------------------------
1. Find the MiSTer's IP address (the MiSTer menu, or your router's list).
2. Open FileZilla: protocol SFTP, host = the MiSTer's IP, user root, password 1, port 22.
3. On the MiSTer side open /media/fat/games/C64/Demos/ and make a new folder there, for example "Octopus build 1".
4. Put "Octopus.prg" and "Octopus.reu" together in that folder (the same name, so the Octophonic browser
   puts the .reu into the REU first, then runs the .prg).
5. Start the Octophonic 64 core, F1 (the browser), Demos, the folder, RETURN on "Octopus.prg".

The .reu is 1 MB (the picture, 120 copper lists, the turn-and-zoom table): the browser shows a load bar, then the screen stays black
for a moment while the 6502 copies it into OctoVIC's memory, and the show begins.

Needs the Octophonic 64 core with OctoVIC 2 or later; a stock C64 gets a message and nothing else.
