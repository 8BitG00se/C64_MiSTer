GREAT WAVE build 2  -  a picture show with music for the Octophonic 64
===========================================================

The Great Wave off Kanagawa, Katsushika Hokusai, c. 1831 - public domain; scan of the print via Wikimedia Commons.
Music: Sōran Bushi, traditional Japanese fishermen's song, arranged for 8 SIDs by Mong00se.

What it is. The whole print as one 256-colour picture, 1024 x 702 pixels, on an OctoVIC bitmap layer that is turned and
sized (zoomed) by the machine itself. The show loops, about 51 seconds, and the music loops with it (new in build 2: the song on all 8 SIDs - taiko, rim and
clicks, a rowing bass, koto, a shakuhachi-like lead and its answer, a pad, and the sea, kept low under the melody):

  - black screen, then a raster wipe: the print appears from the top behind a band of blue and cream colour bars
  - 4 seconds on the whole print, with the title
  - a glide to the left: the boat in the trough under the wave
  - a slow pan (20 seconds) up the wave, along its crest and through the claw-like foam, down to the small Mount Fuji
  - a pause on Mount Fuji, a zoom into it (2x), a hold, and a zoom back out to the whole print
  - the credit line, then a wipe out, and round again

Keys:  SPACE pauses (the music goes quiet) and goes on.   D shows the debug line (LATE = OctoVIC lines that were late last frame, 0 is right;
MAX, TOT (since reset), OVR = frames the 6502 was too slow for).   F7 silences the SIDs and quits to BASIC.

PUTTING IT ON THE MISTER (FILEZILLA)
------------------------------------
1. Find the MiSTer's IP address (the MiSTer menu, or your router's list).
2. Open FileZilla: protocol SFTP, host = the MiSTer's IP, user root, password 1, port 22.
3. On the MiSTer side open /media/fat/games/C64/Demos/ and make a new folder there, for example "Great Wave build 2".
4. Put "Great Wave.prg" and "Great Wave.reu" together in that folder (the same name, so the Octophonic browser
   puts the .reu into the REU first, then runs the .prg).
5. Start the Octophonic 64 core, F1 (the browser), Demos, the folder, RETURN on "Great Wave.prg".

The .reu is 2.0 MB (the picture, and the music: 2550 frames of SID registers, played from the REU): the browser shows a load bar, then the screen stays black for about a second while the 6502 copies
the picture into OctoVIC's memory, and the show begins.

"OctoPaint Pictures build 2.d64" has two pictures for OctoPaint, each a 320 x 200 picture with black bars at the sides:
"STARRY NIGHT.OCT" (Vincent van Gogh, 1889) and "GREAT WAVE.OCT". Put it in /media/fat/games/C64/Disks/, mount it in the
OSD (drive 8) and Open in OctoPaint.

Needs the Octophonic 64 core with OctoVIC 2 or later; a stock C64 gets a message and nothing else.

Made by Mong00se. 6502 (ACME); gen.py prepares the data on a PC. github.com/8BitG00se/C64_MiSTer, tools/greatwave.
