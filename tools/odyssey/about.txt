2026: AN OCTOPHONIC ODYSSEY - build @N@ - by Mong00se

Image (Octophonic 64): the "Cosmic Cliffs" in the Carina Nebula, from the James Webb Space Telescope - NASA, ESA, CSA, STScI.
Music: "The Blue Danube" (An der schoenen blauen Donau, 1866) by Johann Strauss II, arranged by Mong00se.

One program for three machines - it looks at the machine it is on:
- Octophonic 64 (OctoVIC, 8 SIDs, REU): the picture in 256 colours, 2048 x 1186, a slow camera through the
  cliffs, the waltz on all 8 SIDs (strings, harp, horns, flute, bells, cellos, timpani), twinkling stars,
  sparkles, a comet, 8 equaliser bars.
- C64 with a second SID (at $D420, $D500 or $DE00): our own Andromeda-style spiral galaxy (no photo) in hires,
  turned and drawn anew by the 6502, with ~100 live stars orbiting in its arms (the inner ones faster), twinkling
  stars, glints, a scroll text and an equaliser, the waltz in stereo on 2 SIDs.
- Stock C64: the same galaxy, the waltz on 1 SID.
Everything you see and hear is done by the 6502 on the machine.

Run it
- Octophonic (MiSTer): run "Install to MiSTer.bat", then pick "Octophonic Odyssey.prg" in the Octophonic browser
  (it loads the .reu beside it). From the .d64 it works too: the program asks Octophonic Main for its .reu.
- A real C64 or an emulator: the .d64 in drive 8, LOAD"*",8,1 then RUN. Leave the disk in: the pictures load
  from it as the show goes (each takes about 40 seconds on a stock 1541 - the music plays on).

The source is on the disk for OCTOCODE: ODYSSEY.ASM and its parts PLAYER, SHOW, FX, IRIS, OCTO, OCTOFX, CAMERA
and SONG. F3 to load a part, edit, F2 to build and run. F2 on any part builds the whole demo. The parts must also be beside the disk on
the SD card (the .bat copies them there) - that is where the compile finds them. Keep 50 or more blocks free
on the disk: F5 writes a part (and the program) anew before the old copy goes.
