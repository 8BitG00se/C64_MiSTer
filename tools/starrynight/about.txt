STARRY NIGHT build 2  -  a picture show with music for the Octophonic 64
================================================================

The Starry Night, Vincent van Gogh, 1889 - public domain; scan of the painting, via Wikimedia Commons.
Music: Gymnopédie No. 1, Erik Satie, 1888, arranged for 8 SIDs by Mong00se

What it is. The whole painting as one 256-colour picture, 1024 x 816 pixels, on an OctoVIC bitmap layer that is turned and
sized (zoomed) by the machine itself. The show loops, about 50 seconds, and the music loops with it (new in build 2: Satie's piece on all 8 SIDs - melody, bass, chords,
a string pad, harp, horns, soft timpani and a high shimmer like the stars; the SIDs come in one at a time as the painting
appears, swell as the camera zooms into the moon, and settle as the show wipes out):

  - black screen, then a raster wipe: the painting appears from the top behind a band of colour bars
  - 4 seconds on the whole painting, with the title (the harp comes in; the melody follows a few seconds later)
  - a glide to the left stars, then a slow pan over the big swirl to the moon (22 seconds)
  - zoom into the moon (2x), hold, zoom back out to the whole painting
  - the credit line, then a wipe out, and round again

Keys:  SPACE pauses (the music goes quiet) and goes on.   D shows the debug line (LATE = OctoVIC lines that were late last frame, 0 is right;
MAX, TOT (since reset), OVR = frames the 6502 was too slow for).   F7 silences the SIDs and quits to BASIC.

PUTTING IT ON THE MISTER (FILEZILLA)
------------------------------------
1. Find the MiSTer's IP address (the MiSTer menu, or your router's list).
2. Open FileZilla: protocol SFTP, host = the MiSTer's IP, user root, password 1, port 22.
3. On the MiSTer side open /media/fat/games/C64/Demos/ and make a new folder there, for example "Starry Night build 2".
4. Put "Starry Night.prg" and "Starry Night.reu" together in that folder (the same name, so the Octophonic browser
   puts the .reu into the REU first, then runs the .prg).
5. Start the Octophonic 64 core, F1 (the browser), Demos, the folder, RETURN on "Starry Night.prg".

The .reu is 2.0 MB (the painting, and the music: 2520 frames of SID registers, played from the REU): the browser shows a load bar, then the screen stays black for about a second while the 6502 copies
the picture into OctoVIC's memory, and the show begins.

Needs the Octophonic 64 core with OctoVIC 2 or later; a stock C64 gets a message and nothing else.

Made by Mong00se. 6502 (ACME); gen.py prepares the data on a PC. github.com/8BitG00se/C64_MiSTer, tools/starrynight.
