THE LAST SUPPER - for the Octophonic 64 (OctoVIC 2+, 8 SIDs, REU). By Mong00se.

Leonardo da Vinci's Last Supper (1495-1498, the wall of Santa Maria delle Grazie in Milan) as one 256-colour picture,
revealed by a raster wipe in the fresco's colours. The camera goes in to Christ framed by the window, to his face, then
walks slowly along the table past the twelve - three by three either side of him - comes back to Judas with his money
bag, down to the bread on the table, up to the coffered ceiling, and back out to the whole wall. All of it done live by
the 6502 and OctoVIC's turned-and-sized bitmap layer.

The music: "Ave Maria ... virgo serena" by Josquin des Prez (c. 1485; Josquin was in Milan in those years), its first
two verses, arranged for 8 SIDs by Mong00se from Josquin's own four voices: a small chapel - a choir of pulse waves
(each voice two singers), a chamber organ and its pedal, recorders, a small bell. The voices come in one after another,
as Josquin wrote them.

Run it: put The Last Supper.prg and The Last Supper.reu in the same folder (or run "Install to MiSTer.bat"), then pick
The Last Supper.prg in the Octophonic browser - it loads the .reu with it.

The painting: public domain (the high-resolution photograph, via Wikimedia Commons). The notes of the Ave Maria: the
Josquin Research Project's transcription (New Josquin Edition 23.6), https://josquin.stanford.edu, CC BY-NC 4.0.
